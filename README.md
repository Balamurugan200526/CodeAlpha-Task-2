## 📖 About the Project

This project is **Task 2** of the CodeAlpha Cybersecurity Internship. The goal was to create an educational module focused on phishing attacks — explaining how they work, how to recognize them, and how to stay safe.

Instead of a static PowerPoint presentation, this was built as a fully interactive **HTML web page** that users can open in any browser. It includes animations, a live phishing email demo with red flags highlighted, and a scored interactive quiz for better engagement.

Phishing is one of the most common cyberattacks today:

* **3.4 billion** phishing emails are sent every single day
* **36%** of all data breaches involve phishing
* **$17,700** is lost every minute due to phishing attacks

## 📚 Sections Covered

### Section 01 — What is Phishing?

Explains the concept of phishing, how attackers disguise themselves as trusted entities, and what their goals are.

### Section 02 — Types of Phishing Attacks

Covers all major types of phishing:

|Type|Description|
|-|-|
|Email Phishing|Mass fake emails from banks, services, or companies|
|Spear Phishing|Targeted attack using personal information|
|Smishing|Phishing via SMS/text messages|
|Vishing|Voice phishing via phone calls|
|Whaling|Targeted attacks on executives and CEOs|
|Clone Phishing|Copying a real email and replacing links|

### Section 03 — How to Recognize a Phishing Email

Includes a **live interactive demo** of a fake PayPal phishing email with red flags highlighted in real time:

* Fake sender domain (`paypa1.com` instead of `paypal.com`)
* Generic greeting with no name
* Urgency language and fake deadlines
* Suspicious links pointing to fake domains
* Requests for sensitive information

### Section 04 — Social Engineering Tactics

Explains the 6 psychological manipulation tactics used by attackers:

* **Fear** — Threatening account suspension or security breaches
* **Urgency** — Fake deadlines to prevent careful thinking
* **Greed** — Fake prizes and lottery winnings
* **Trust** — Impersonating known brands, banks, or employers
* **Authority** — Posing as IT support, government agencies
* **Sympathy** — Fake emergency situations asking for help

### Section 05 — Best Practices to Stay Safe

8 actionable tips to protect yourself:

1. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
2. Verify sender email addresses carefully
3. Hover over links before clicking
4. Never share OTPs or passwords
5. Check for HTTPS and correct domain names
6. Keep software and antivirus updated
7. Use a password manager
8. Report suspicious emails immediately

### Section 06 — Interactive Quiz

5-question quiz to test phishing awareness with instant feedback and a final score.

## 🧪 Quiz Details

The interactive quiz contains 5 scenario-based questions:

|#|Topic|
|-|-|
|Q1|Identifying a fake sender domain (typosquatting)|
|Q2|Recognizing urgency tactics in phishing emails|
|Q3|Responding to a vishing (voice phishing) call|
|Q4|Identifying a safe vs. fake URL|
|Q5|Recognizing smishing with greed and urgency tactics|

Each question shows:

* Instant correct/wrong feedback with color coding
* A detailed explanation of why the answer is correct
* A final score out of 5 with a personalized message
